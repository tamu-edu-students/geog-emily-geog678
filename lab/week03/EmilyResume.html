During my time at UTSA, I had valuable experiences, such as internships with the Alamo Trust and the UTSA Center for Archaeological Research. Additionally, I joined the UTSA Honors College. My time at UTSA provided the stepping stone I needed to pursue a career in archaeology.

A few months after graduating from UTSA, I joined Stantec as an archaeology technician. Desiring to further my career prospects by gaining skills in GIS technology, I decided to enroll in the online Master's of Geoscience program with Texas A&M University. I am currently scheduled to graduate in 2026.
